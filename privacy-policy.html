Privacy Policy

KamaTeez is committed to keeping your personal information safe. Whenever you shop with us or use one of our services, you are trusting us with your details, and we take that trust seriously. Protecting your data and your privacy is our top priority.

How Do We Handle Your Data?

  • We tell you why we collect it. How we process your personal data depends on the reason it was collected, and we will always let you know what that reason is.
  • We use it only to serve you better. We use your personal data only to give you a good experience, whether you visit our website or our physical stores. This includes handling the services you ask for, such as processing and delivering your order.
  • We collect only what we need. The information we may process includes your name, address, email address, phone number, payment details, and order history.
  • You stay in control. You have the right to access, correct, delete, and ask questions about your personal data. In certain situations, you may also object to how we use it.

If you have any more questions or if you need further assistance, please contact our friendly customer service team through our email.
