About KamaTeez

KamaTeez is a local startup clothing brand, we sell shirts with art at their core. Our name was greatly inspired by the pin cushion used in sewing, and every pin holds a piece of the craft together. We keep art close to our hearts and put it into everything we make. Our shirts are made from premium fabrics and stitched with care; which are all made to last with you.

We are KamaTeez. Join us, take a pin, and poke along with us.
